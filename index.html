ONE VERIFIED LESSON

Calculate current with Ohm’s law

Atomic skill: find current from voltage and resistance.

I = E ÷ R

Plain English. Current is flow. Voltage is the push. Resistance is the squeeze. Divide the push by the squeeze.
Technician words. Current I is amperes. Voltage E is volts. Resistance R is ohms. Use the same circuit path.
Easy way to remember. Cover I in the triangle. What is left is E over R.
Worked example. A 240 V heater circuit measures 60 ohms. I = 240 ÷ 60 = 4 A.
Safety. This lesson is math and recognition only. It does not tell you to work on live equipment.
